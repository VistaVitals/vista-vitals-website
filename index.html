Services

Monthly Clean Books

Accurate, reconciled financials delivered on schedule. P&L, balance sheet, and cash flow statement — the foundation everything else is built on.

Financial Health Panel

Your monthly diagnostic report covering 5 categories, 10 metrics, status indicators, trend tracking, and written analysis of what the numbers mean for your business.

Quarterly Deep Scan Call

A 1 hour deep financial review of 90-day trends, potential emerging risks, and brainstorming on upcoming decisions.

Payroll Accounting

Integrated with Gusto. Payroll entries, tax liabilities, and benefit accruals flow directly into your books—accurately reflected in each monthly close.

Expense Management

Ramp and Dext integration. Receipts captured, coded, and reconciled seamlessly—keeping spend organized and visible without manual follow-up.

Direct Communication

Clear answers, when you need them. You communicate directly with the team handling your books—ensuring accuracy, continuity, and a smooth monthly close.

Rolling Cash Flow Forecast

A forward-looking 13-week cash flow projection, updated monthly. Move from "here's what happened" to "here's what's coming"—so you're never caught off guard by a cash shortfall or missed opportunity.

Annual Budget + Variance Tracking

We build your annual budget alongside you, then track actual performance against it every month. The most requested CFO-level deliverable small businesses don't have—answering one question: "Am I on track?"

Bank & Lender Readiness

Financials cleaned, formatted, and prepared for loan applications and lines of credit discussions. Includes debt schedule tracking and covenant compliance monitoring where applicable—so you're ready when capital is on the table.

Annual Board & Investor Report

A comprehensive year-end presentation covering your company's financial performance, key metrics, industry trends, and strategic outlook. Built to stand on its own in front of a board, investor group, or advisory team.

Investor & Capital Readiness

Financials structured and presented for equity raises, investor due diligence, and partnership discussions. Includes cap table summary, key metrics deck, use-of-funds breakdown, and historical financial narratives—so when an investor asks for your numbers, you hand them a story that closes.

Buy-Side Acquisition Valuation

Independent valuation analysis for business acquisitions — including normalized EBITDA, comparable transactions, multiples analysis, industry overview, and deal structure review — so you buy at the right price.
